## 🎯 OPTION 1: OBD-II Bluetooth Adapter ⭐ RECOMMENDED

**What is it?**
- Plugs into your car's OBD-II port (under dashboard)
- Reads car data: speed, RPM, fuel, engine info
- Some models have GPS built-in

### **Products to Buy:**

#### **1. ELM327 Bluetooth OBD-II Scanner**
- **Price:** ₹500-1500 ($15-40)
- **Where:** Amazon India, Flipkart
- **Search:** "ELM327 Bluetooth OBD2"
- **Features:** 
  - ✅ Reads speed, RPM
  - ✅ Works with most cars (1996+)
  - ✅ Bluetooth connection to laptop
  - ❌ No GPS (need phone GPS too)

**Amazon India Links to Search:**
- "ELM327 Bluetooth OBD2 Scanner"
- "Vgate iCar 2 Bluetooth OBD2"
- "BAFX Products Bluetooth OBD2"

## 🔧 Installation Guide

### **OBD-II Device:**
1. Find OBD-II port (under dashboard, near steering)
2. Plug device in
3. Turn car ignition ON
4. Wait for LED to light up
5. Connect via Bluetooth to laptop/phone

### **Hardwired GPS:**
1. **Don't DIY!** - Get professional installation
2. Cost: ₹500-1500
3. Takes: 30-60 minutes
4. Connects to car battery
